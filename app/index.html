I am a post-doctoral fellow in Mechanical Engineering at the Colorado School of Mines. I additionally hold a visiting post-doc position at Northwestern University. My research combines experimental and modeling-based techniques to study deformation mechanisms in metals.

This blog showcases my research and other interests.

Research View Outline

Exploring interaction between the deformation mechanisms in shape memory alloys (SMAs) using experimental and modeling techniques.

Modeling SMA mechanical response from nanometer to macro scale using micromechanical and phenomenological models.

Characterizing microstrictural phenomena in metals using high energy diffraction microscopy (HEDM).
