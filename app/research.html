A research effort that combines theoretical and empirical components has higher scientific impact on the community.

Multiscale modeling coupling multiple deformation modes in metals

Understanding the mechanisms of interaction between deformation modes in metals is key from basic science as well as application perspective. I have developed a microstructural framework that can simulate coupled phase transformation, twinning and slip. I have experience in developing and implementing phenomenological macro-scale elasto-plastic models that can simulate deformation under complex loading conditions.

X-ray diffraction based techniques to reconstruct 3D microstructure

High energy diffraction microscopy (HEDM) is an X-ray based technique to reconstruct 3D grain-scale microstrure in bulk metallic specimen. I am using HEDM along with microstructural modeling to characterize subgrain scale deformation in advanced alloys. This approach can be used to characterize microstructure evolution in low-symmetry, phase transforming and twinning materials.

My goal is to develop a research program that draws from microstructural modeling, diffraction-based reconstruction techniques and modern data analysis methods to characterize microstructural evolution in materials that deform by multiple modes.
